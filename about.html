These policies exist because compliance shouldn't block small companies from building great products. Startups deserve access to the same quality policies that enterprises pay consultants thousands of dollars to create.

Why Open Source?

Most compliance policy templates cost $5,000-$20,000 from consultants. That's a real barrier for startups already stretched thin. By open-sourcing these policies, I hope to level the playing field.

The CC-BY-SA-4.0 license ensures these stay free. You can use them commercially, modify them, and even build a business around them. Just share improvements back to the community.
